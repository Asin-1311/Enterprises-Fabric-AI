> | High | 60 |

## 8. chk-08

**Question** (asked by S03): How are government-guaranteed advances treated for NPA classification on our bank branch checklist?

**Gold answer:** Central Government guaranteed advances (guarantee not invoked) are standard for classification but NPA for income recognition; State Government guaranteed advances overdue more than 90 days are NPA for both.

**Gold documents:** CHK-BANK-01

**Drafting note:** CHK-BANK-01 section B.

**`CHK-BANK-01:B:1`** (Checklist: Bank Branch Audit - Advances and NPA Classification; version 3.0; effective 2025-03-01; section B; `data/corpus/checklists/bank_branch_audit_checklist.md`)

> Checklist: Bank Branch Audit - Advances and NPA Classification > B. NPA classification checks
> - [ ] Term loans: interest or principal overdue for more than 90 days classified as NPA.
> - [ ] Cash credit / overdraft: accounts "out of order" classified as NPA. Out of order
>       means the balance is continuously above the sanctioned limit or drawing power, OR
>       there are no credits for 90 days continuously as on the balance sheet date, OR the
>       credits are not enough to cover the interest debited in that period.
> - [ ] Bills purchased and discounted overdue for more than 90 days classified as NPA.
> - [ ] Classification is borrower-wise: if one facility of a borrower is NPA, all
>       facilities of that borrower are NPA.
> - [ ] Classification is based on record of recovery; security value or net worth of the
>       borrower or guarantor is NOT taken into account.
> - [ ] Accounts regularised by one or a few credits just before year end reviewed for
>       inherent weakness and for reversals just after year end.
> - [ ] Central Government guaranteed advances (guarantee not invoked) treated as standard
>       for classification but NPA for income recognition; State Government guaranteed
>       advances overdue more than 90 days treated as NPA for both.

**`CHK-BANK-01:C:1`** (Checklist: Bank Branch Audit - Advances and NPA Classification; version 3.0; effective 2025-03-01; section C; `data/corpus/checklists/bank_branch_audit_checklist.md`)

> Checklist: Bank Branch Audit - Advances and NPA Classification > C. Sub-classification and provisioning
> - [ ] Substandard (NPA for up to 12 months): 15%.
> - [ ] Doubtful up to 1 year (D1): 25% of secured portion + 100% of unsecured portion.
> - [ ] Doubtful 1 to 3 years (D2): 40% of secured portion + 100% of unsecured portion.
> - [ ] Doubtful more than 3 years (D3): 100% + 100%.
> - [ ] Loss assets: 100%.

## 9. tpl-03

**Question** (asked by P01): According to our auditor's report template, which opinion applies when a misstatement is material and pervasive?

**Gold answer:** An adverse opinion (SA 705). A material but not pervasive misstatement leads to a qualified opinion.

**Gold documents:** TPL-AR-01

**Drafting note:** TPL-AR-01 section 2 table. Decision support only: the engagement partner decides the opinion.

**`TPL-AR-01:2:1`** (Template: Auditor's Report Formats (SA 700 / 705 / 706); version 1.0; effective 2024-04-01; section 2; `data/corpus/templates/audit_report_formats.md`)

> Template: Auditor's Report Formats (SA 700 / 705 / 706) > 2. Choosing a modified opinion (SA 705)
> A modification is needed when the financial statements as a whole are not free from
> material misstatement, or when sufficient appropriate audit evidence cannot be obtained.
> | Situation | Material, not pervasive | Material and pervasive |
> |---|---|---|
> | Misstatement found (evidence obtained) | Qualified opinion | Adverse opinion |
> | Unable to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence | Qualified opinion | Disclaimer of opinion |

**`TPL-AR-01:3:1`** (Template: Auditor's Report Formats (SA 700 / 705 / 706); version 1.0; effective 2024-04-01; section 3; `data/corpus/templates/audit_report_formats.md`)

> Template: Auditor's Report Formats (SA 700 / 705 / 706) > 3. Emphasis of Matter paragraph (SA 706)
> Include one only when the matter is fundamental to users' understanding, the opinion would
> not need to be modified for it, and (where SA 701 applies) it is not a key audit matter.
> Put it in a separate section headed "Emphasis of Matter", refer to the relevant disclosure,
> and state that the opinion is not modified in respect of the matter. Examples: uncertainty
> over exceptional litigation, a significant subsequent event, early application of a new
> accounting standard, a major catastrophe.

> Northwind Textiles: Materiality Working FY 2025-26 > 1. Benchmark
> Policy: Materiality Policy v2.0 (effective 1 April 2025), manufacturing benchmark 5% of
> profit before tax. Profit is stable, so the revenue fallback is not used.
